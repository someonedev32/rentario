1) Quick Summary

instairent Pro is designed to run offline. Your rent-a-car business data (e.g., vehicles, customers, reservations) is stored locally on your device.

  • No selling of personal data.
  • No cross-app tracking (we do not use data for advertising tracking).
  • Payments are handled by Apple through In-App Purchases.

2) Data We Collect

The App is primarily on-device. Depending on how subscriptions are validated by Apple, limited information may be processed:

A. On-device business data (stored locally)

  • Vehicles, availability notes, and reservation records
  • Customer details you enter
  • Status fields (e.g., confirmed / active / completed) and internal notes

This data stays on the device unless you explicitly export/share it using your device’s sharing features.

B. Purchase / subscription information

  • Subscription status and purchase history related to Apple In-App Purchases (e.g., active/expired)
  • Receipt/transaction data needed to unlock premium access

C. Identifiers (limited, for purchase validation)

  • Identifiers used by Apple/StoreKit for purchase validation and fraud prevention

We do not request or collect sensitive categories (e.g., precise location, contacts, health data) unless a future feature explicitly requires it. If we add such a feature, we will update this policy.

3) How We Use Data

  • App functionality: to create and manage bookings, vehicles, and customer records on your device.
  • Purchase validation: to confirm subscription access, restore purchases, and handle entitlement checks.
  • Support: to respond if you contact us (only what you choose to send).
  • Security: to prevent abuse and protect the App where applicable.

4) Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your data. We do not share your on-device business data with third parties. Subscription purchases and billing are processed by Apple.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — processes payments and manages subscriptions.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

5) Subscriptions & Payments

instairent Pro offers a 7-day free trial. After the trial, the App offers subscription plans:

  • €29.99 / 6 months
  • €49.99 / 12 months

Billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID / App Store subscription settings.

If you start a free trial, it converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before the trial ends (per Apple’s subscription rules).

6) Data Retention

On-device business data remains on your device until you delete it (e.g., remove records in the App or uninstall the App). Purchase-related records are maintained by Apple as part of App Store transactions.

7) Security

We use reasonable safeguards in the App design. However, no method of storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device (passcode/Face ID) and backups.

8) Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because the App stores business data locally, you can typically exercise these rights directly within the App by editing or deleting records.

For subscription billing and refunds, use Apple’s subscription management tools.

For support requests, contact us at info@instaserver.net.

9) Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10) Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Effective date” above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice within the App or on our website.
