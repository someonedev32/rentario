1) What this covers

Rentario Pro is an offline booking management app for rent-a-car businesses. This policy covers the information processed when you use the App, including how your data is stored and how subscriptions are handled.

2) Data collection

We do not collect data from the App in the sense of transmitting your app data off the device to our servers for storage or profiling. Your reservations, vehicles, and customer records remain on your device.

Note: Apple may process App Store purchase and subscription information as part of providing billing and subscription services (see Section 4).

3) On-device data

The App stores the following information locally on your device (examples):

  • Reservations / bookings (dates, vehicle, status, notes)
  • Fleet information (vehicles and related details)
  • Customer information you enter

This data is not uploaded to our servers. If you use device features to export, share, print, or back up information, that action is controlled by you and your device/Apple settings.

4) Subscriptions & payments

Rentario Pro may offer auto-renewable subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple via In-App Purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.

  • Apple manages billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds.
  • You can manage subscriptions in: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Apple may process transaction and subscription status information to provide these services under Apple’s own policies.

5) Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share your on-device business data with third parties.

The only third party involved for subscriptions is Apple, which processes payments and subscription management through the App Store.

6) Retention

Your business data stays on your device until you delete it (e.g., delete records inside the App) or uninstall the App.

App Store purchase records are retained by Apple as part of your App Store account history.

7) Security

We design the App to store data locally. No system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device (passcode/Face ID) and managing backups.

8) Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since your business data is stored locally, you can typically exercise these rights by editing or deleting records within the App.

If you need assistance related to the App itself, contact us at contact@flamurahmeti.com. For billing/refunds, use Apple’s subscription and refund tools.

9) Children

Rentario Pro is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Effective date” at the top of this page. If changes are material, we may also provide notice within the App or on our website.

Please also review our Terms & Conditions .
